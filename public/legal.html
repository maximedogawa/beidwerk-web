02

Privacy

What this site keeps

  • No cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising.
  • Nothing comes from other companies. The fonts, pictures, songs and videos are served from this site's own server; your browser talks to no one else while you are here.
  • No log of your visits. The server does not record who opens which page.
  • One setting in your browser. If you switch between the light and the dark look, your browser remembers that choice on your own device (local storage). Nothing is sent anywhere; you can clear it in your browser at any time.
  • E-mail. If you write to us, your message and address are used only to answer you.
  • Links to other sites (the live apps, GitHub, X) lead to sites with their own rules.

Under the GDPR you may ask what we know about you, and have it corrected or deleted: write to the address above. You may also complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde).
