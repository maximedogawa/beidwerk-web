Die Wiener Wörter

Wienerisch English Why
Bursch my lad The label man's patronising pat on the head; same word as in Zwei Kaffee ("lad"), kept apart from Burschi = "kiddo".
bevor's dich trifft it won't come round again The salesman's pressure line; "won't come round again" is the classic hard sell and rhymes with "pen".
is so bequem it's so comfy Viennese "is" for "ist"; "comfy" is the lazy, cosy word everyone uses for the thing that eats them.
I hab's g'lesen I read it all through Clipped dialect past tense; plain spoken English.
Lass di ned verwahren Don't let them keep you safe "verwahren" is safekeeping, custody; "keep you safe" is exactly what Sorglos promises, so the warning bites.
ned don't The Viennese "nicht"; no dialect left in English.

Nicht für dich. Mit dir.
