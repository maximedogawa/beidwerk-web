Die Wiener Wörter

Wienerisch English Why
Burschi kiddo Fixed across the album; gruff and fond in one word, two syllables like "Burschi", stress on the first.
Hör auf den Takt Stick to the beat "Listen to the beat" is too long; "stick to" is how a bandleader actually says it.
nix davon not one bit of it "nix" is the blunt Viennese nothing; "not one bit of it" keeps the shrug and the syllables.
I kann's ned Can't do it "ned" = not; dropping the "I" keeps it as clipped as the dialect.
a schönes Wort a lovely word "schön" in this mouth is soft and a little wry; "lovely" carries both.
Werkzeug und Geduld a toolbox and patience STAMM's bar-owner practicality; "toolbox" is concrete and sings on the stress.

Nicht für dich. Mit dir.
