Die Wiener Wörter

Wienerisch English Why
schlecht g'launt got the hump British for sulking in a bad mood; one punchy syllable to land on before "Lovely".
Herrlich Lovely The dry, delighted one-word verdict; British "lovely" carries the same irony.
Typ bloke The pub word for "some guy".
a Schas rubbish As the brief: dismissive, fun to spit, sits on the downbeat.
des war no a Party now that was a party The old-timer's nostalgia formula; reused for "now that was a year".
Klo the bog Working-class British for the toilet; "the bog's a crime scene" is pure pub.
Is nix los / Es is alles los Nothing's happening / it's all happening Keeps the mirror joke: the same phrase flipped, and "it's all happening" is a real cheer.
glaubt's mir listen here The streetwise "trust me"; it rhymes with "right here".
tanzt's dance Viennese plural imperative; English imperative needs nothing more.
ka no The Viennese "kein"; plain "no", or "all gone" where it sings better.
Kuss snog Cheekier than "kiss" and on the same single beat.
g'raunzt / raunzen moaning / moan Per the brief, raunzen = moan; "moan tomorrow, dance tonight" keeps the chant's later/now swing and the syllables.
Ja Yeah The deadpan answer to the last moan.
