Language · Viennese first, then English

How we speak

Warm, plain, short. Verbs before nouns, one idea per line, never a lecture. German is always "du". Both languages are written, not translated.

01

The band's voice

Together the band speaks Viennese: warm, dry, a little rude, never cynical. Short sentences. Jokes about itself, never about the audience. No slogans, no speeches about big tech, no marketing words.

03

The products' voice

The products speak like the band at its calmest: plain words, the verb first, no crypto words, no urgency.

Instead of Say (EN) Sag (DE)

Transaction confirmed Both of you confirmed it. Ihr habt es beide bestätigt.

05

Two languages

  1. Austrian German first. Songs, band lines, posters and the first draft of every text are written in Viennese.
  2. English second, written fresh. A singable or readable English version, not a word-for-word copy; a literal translation only for subtitles.
  3. Keep the untranslatable (leiwand, Beisl, Melange, Burschi) and explain it once if needed.
  4. Names never change: ECHT, STAMM, BOGEN, SCHLAG, Beidwerk, Freiregister, Café Ewig.
