The apps

Every Beidwerk app follows the same idea: two parties come together, and both confirm what happened. A client and a freelancer, a buyer and a seller, a wallet and an app. Each app has its own mark, made from two of the four signs, and its own accent colour.

04

One grammar

Pair, contact, trace, settle

Every app works the same way. Two parties approach each other, they meet (shown as a spark or two small ticks), the moment is recorded, and the app comes to rest. The playful part stays in the marks and the moment of contact; everything around it is calm. That is why a wallet, a register and a payment app look like one family.
