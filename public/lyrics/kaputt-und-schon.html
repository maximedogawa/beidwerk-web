Kaputt und Schön

Broken and Beautiful · in German and in English

← the album

ECHT sings, alone, his first morning: wet, laughing, wide-eyed, rain turning to low sun; fast and bouncy, 133.9 bpm; the 4:12 opening and "All right, point five" are half-spoken.

I've got nothing with me but a cassette and a list made out of paper.

The Viennese words

Wienerisch English Why
nix nothing The street "nix"; "got nothing with me" keeps the shrug.
a Hand a hand The dialect article; plain English already sounds spoken.
Na gut All right The give-in sigh before the last point; "all right" sits on the same two beats.
herkomm / renn / lach come from / chase / laughing Clipped spoken verbs; "'Cause where I come from" keeps the casual run.
