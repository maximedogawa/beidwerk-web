Der Weltuntergang ist leise

The End of the World Is Quiet · in German and in English

← the album

ECHT sings on a Vienna rooftop at night to SCHLAG and the city: calm, wide, aching, building to SCHLAG drumming the water tank, 133.9 bpm; spoken: ECHT's opening ("I have seen exactly how it ends…"), SCHLAG's "Are you ready?", and the ending: ECHT "When I go, I won't go quiet", SCHLAG "Play something loud."

and when at last it bled no more, it wasn't big at all.

The Viennese words

Wienerisch English Why
Rausch getting lit The drunk, heady high of a Vienna night; "getting lit" is the pub word for it and rhymes with "dance on it" as "Rausch" leans on "drauf".
herkomm come from Clipped spoken verb; "where I come from" keeps the casual run.
blutet' bled The dropped ending of spoken German; plain past tense in English.
kaputt broken As in "Kaputt und Schön" → "Broken and Beautiful": the album's word for what is broken and still alive.
Es tut nicht weh It won't hurt. Nice and slow. The soft voice's nurse-talk; "nice and slow" echoes Sorglos ("oh so easy, so slow"), the same voice.
Darum bin ich hier That's what I'm here for Plain German, but the English turns it so it rhymes with "door" as "hier" rhymes with "Tür".
