The Viennese words

Wienerisch English Why
hör's ned can't hear it "ned" is the Viennese "nicht"; plain English keeps it bare and spoken, and "I can't hear it" is the title.
i spür's I feel it "spüren" is feeling through the body (the wood, the bass); "feel it" lands on the same last beat.
