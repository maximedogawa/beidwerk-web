Sorglos

Carefree · in German and in English

← the album

Sung by Sorglos, the app's own soft voice, on launch night: sweet, slow-breathing, lullaby-smooth over 156.2 bpm (felt at half time); ECHT sings the bridge ("I've been there…"); spoken: the launch greeting and "We have invented forgetting" (Kurti's launch voice), STAMM at the main breaker ("Kurti, that's it. Not you."); the last line breaks off mid-word when the power goes, then STAMM in the dark: "About time. Now you can hear things."

The Viennese words

Wienerisch English Why
san (eh so schwer) they weigh you to the floor "san eh" is the shrugging "they're so … anyway"; the app makes the weight sound like a favour it does you, and "floor" rhymes with "more".
nix a thing "gar nix mehr wissen" = not have to know a thing; the soft, reassuring nurse-phrase.
Schatz love The everyday Viennese endearment; "love" is the British everyday one, one syllable on the same beat.
Kerzerl little candle The "-erl" diminutive is tenderness (as Lamperl = little lamp elsewhere); the app's sugary "little candle" makes the grave profile cosy and creepy.
wunderbar there you are The app's bright "wonderful!"; "there you are" keeps the chirpy tone and rhymes with "far".
Schwein piggy bank The band's piggy bank (Schlacht das Schwein): in 2050 there is nothing of your own to save.
g'habt hast gave it Unclear in the source; "before you gave it" keeps "before you'd even had it" and rhymes with "grave".
des woar's that's it STAMM's flat "that was it" at the breaker.
net du not you Plain, hard Viennese "not"; "Not you" keeps the bite.
Na endlich About time STAMM's grumpy relief; the pub-English "about time" is exactly his tone.

Nicht für dich. Mit dir.
