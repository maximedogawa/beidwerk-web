The Viennese words

Wienerisch English Why
wie geschmiert nice and smooth "Runs like it's greased"; "nice and smooth" is the easy spoken version and rhymes with "move".
is is Dialect "ist"; plain English.
g'schaut seen "geschaut" = looked/seen; plain English, the swagger is in "a lovelier sight than this crowd".
kracht und scheppert crashes, rattles The noise words of a dive band: hard consonants on the beat.
schreib ma frei we'll write brand new "ma" = "wir"; side B is blank and ours, "brand new" rhymes with "through".
na nah The Viennese "no"; STAMM's word for everything, and "nah" rhymes with "are" in London English.
na danke nah, cheers The sarcastic "no thanks"; British "cheers" is thanks with an eye-roll.
a Tier a beast "Spielt wie a Tier" = plays like an animal; "a beast" is the drummer compliment.
Maschin machine ECHT's clipped dialect form; plain English.
Wir! Us! The shout-back; "Us!" is one hard syllable like "Wir!", the way a crowd answers.
echt real "Echt" is also ECHT's name; "something's still real" keeps the meaning (the pun is lost in English).
