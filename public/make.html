The open studio · free to use

Make with it

Beidwerk is made in the open. Its fonts, colours and components are free to use, so your own site or app can stand in the same world, with your own content and an accent colour of its own.

01

Take the look

Three files, one page

1 · Get the files. The beidwerk-web repository on GitHub holds them in site/: the fonts (Archivo and IBM Plex Mono, both under open licences), the design tokens (beidwerk.css: colours, type, spacing, in light and dark) and the kit (kit.css).

2 · Link them.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="beidwerk/fonts/fonts.css">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="beidwerk/beidwerk.css">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="beidwerk/kit.css">
<body class="bw" data-theme="light">   <!-- light = the receipt, dark = the stage -->

3 · Build with the kit. Headings, buttons, cards, tables, tags and notices take the bw- classes (bw-display, bw-button, bw-card, bw-table, bw-tag, bw-notice…). Colour, type and spacing come from the tokens, never by hand.

03

Make it yours

What keeps it Beidwerk

  • Your own accent. Your product changes the colour, never the rules.
  • Two things meet. Show the moment of contact: a confirmation, a handshake, a payment arriving.
  • Calm around, play in the marks. Let the playful part be small and the rest quiet.
  • No text in pictures. Headlines, prices and names are set in type, over or beside the picture.
04

For agents

An MCP server, plain JSON

Agents can work in the Beidwerk style too. The Beidwerk MCP server is read-only and runs on your own machine, with no keys and no costs. One command connects it to Claude Code:

claude mcp add beidwerk -- uvx --from git+https://github.com/maximedogawa/beidwerk-web beidwerk-mcp
Tool What it gives
design_principles the seven principles, the test for anything new, what Beidwerk never does (English or German)
design_tokens colours for the stage or the receipt, type, spacing, radius and lines
kit the three stylesheets to link and every bw- component with an HTML example
signs the four red signs with their meaning in the B and their SVG
build_prompt an image prompt in the Beidwerk style for any subject: light (the receipt) or night (the stage)

The same data is on this site for agents that only read the web: api/beidwerk.json and llms.txt.

05

How Beidwerk itself is made

By hand, with AI tools

Beidwerk is made by one person with a set of AI tools, step by step: every picture, every shot and every cut is chosen by hand, and most are thrown away.

  • The band. Four characters began as concept art. Their faces are drawn in Midjourney from the same references every time, so a close-up and a wide shot show the same person.
  • The songs. Written in Austrian German, sung with Suno, each with a singable English version.
  • The music videos. Planned shot by shot on the song: one realistic still per shot, brought to life as a short moving shot, cut on the beat, one colour grade per video. Where a member sings, the mouth follows the real voice of the song.
  • The apps. Designed in the same language and built one at a time, from the first idea to live.
